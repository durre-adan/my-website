HEC Standardized 5-Year DPT Scheme of Study

The curriculum balances basic medical sciences, therapeutic skills, and supervised clinical practice.

The National Curriculum Revision Committee (NCRC) of HEC regulates the DPT syllabus across Pakistan to align with global standards.

Year-by-Year Academic Breakdown

Years 1 & 2: Basic Sciences

Anatomy, Physiology, Kinesiology, Biochemistry, Pathology, Pharmacology, and Medical Physics.

Years 3 & 4: Clinical Core

Electrotherapy, Therapeutic Exercises, Biomechanics, Diagnostic Imaging, Musculoskeletal & Neurological PT.

Year 5: Advanced Specialties & Internship

Cardiopulmonary PT, Geriatrics, Pediatrics, Emergency Care, Supervised Clinical Practice, Capstone Project.

Supervised Clinical Practice

Students undergo hands-on hospital rotations from Semester 5 through Semester 10 in outpatient departments (OPD), intensive care units (ICU), and rehabilitation wards.
