Regulatory Bodies & Accreditation Landscape

Standardizing clinical practice and university accreditation remains a core focus of educational bodies.

Regulation ensures educational quality control and ethical clinical practice across all healthcare disciplines.

Current Regulatory Pillars

  • Higher Education Commission (HEC): Regulates degree equivalence, university approvals, and the national standardized curriculum.
  • Pakistan Physical Therapy Association (PPTA): Represents physical therapists nationally and internationally, advocating for legal practice standards.
  • Pakistan Physical Therapy Council (PPTC - Proposed): An independent federal council bill aimed at establishing mandatory professional licensing and clinical registration.

Importance of Council Legislation

Establishing the Pakistan Physical Therapy Council (PPTC) will eliminate quackery, enforce standardized licensing, and protect public health.
