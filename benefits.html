Scope, Career Benefits & Job Opportunities

DPT graduates enjoy career flexibility in both domestic and international markets.

DPT offers diverse career paths within government healthcare facilities, private practice, academia, and overseas job markets.

Career Pathways in Pakistan

  • Government Sector Jobs: DPT graduates are eligible for Grade-17 public sector hospital appointments as Physiotherapists.
  • Private Practice: Freedom to open independent physical therapy setups and rehabilitation centers.
  • Academia & Research: Teaching roles as Lecturers, Assistant Professors, and Clinical Demonstrators in universities.
  • Corporate & Ergonomics: Workplace wellness advisors for corporate organizations to prevent repetitive strain injuries.

International Opportunities

Pakistani DPT graduates can sit for licensing exams worldwide, including the NPTE in the USA, FCCPT credentialing, DHA/MOH in the Middle East, and HCPC registration in the United Kingdom.
