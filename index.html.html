Overview of Physical Therapy Education in Pakistan

Physical therapy provides evidence-based rehabilitation to restore functional movement and quality of life.

Physical Therapy (Physiotherapy) in Pakistan has transformed from a basic diploma level program in the mid-20th century into a prestigious, entry-level clinical doctorate: the Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT). This 5-year academic degree equips graduates to act as autonomous healthcare practitioners capable of assessing, diagnosing, and treating physical impairments.

What is DPT?

The Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) is a 5-year post-higher secondary (10 semester) degree program accredited by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan. It trains students to handle musculoskeletal, neurological, cardiopulmonary, and pediatric physical rehabilitation.

Core Objectives of the DPT Program

  • Produce primary healthcare providers in physical rehabilitation.
  • Promote non-pharmacological pain management and functional movement restoration.
  • Instill research-backed clinical decision-making and evidence-based practice.
  • Meet international standards for physical therapy practice and education.

Past Milestone

Established in 1956 with WHO support at JPMC Karachi as a 2-year diploma course.

Present Status

Over 150+ universities offer HEC-standardized 5-year DPT degrees across Pakistan.

Future Horizon

Integration of AI diagnostics, robotic rehabilitation, and telerehabilitation care.
