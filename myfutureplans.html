My Future Plans

A page about where I'd like this degree — and this life — to go next. Some of this is settled, some of it is still a work in progress, which feels about right for this stage.

Short term: finishing the degree well

Over the next two years, my main goal is straightforward — finish my Computer Science degree with a solid grasp of the fundamentals, not just good grades. I'm particularly keen to go deeper into web development and databases, and to take on at least one internship before I graduate.

Medium term: early career

After graduating, I'd like to work as a software developer, ideally somewhere I get to keep learning rather than repeat the same task indefinitely. I'm especially interested in web and product-focused roles, since building things people actually use is what pulled me toward this field in the first place.

Long term: a few honest goals

Longer term, I'd like to either specialise deeply in one area of software development or eventually build something of my own — I haven't decided which yet, and I'm comfortable with that being an open question for now. Whatever direction it takes, I'd like to keep some version of this habit: writing things down, reflecting on them, and updating the plan as I go.
