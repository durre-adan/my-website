About Me

A short introduction to who I am outside of the classroom — my personality, the things I'm curious about, and how I like to spend a free Saturday.

The short version

I'm a full-time student currently in my second year, splitting my time between lectures, part-time freelance work, and far too many browser tabs. I grew up in a small town, moved for university, and I'm slowly getting used to city life, one bus route at a time.

I'd describe myself as curious more than clever — I tend to learn best by breaking things apart and rebuilding them, which is roughly how I ended up enjoying web development.

Things I enjoy

Outside of coursework, I read more non-fiction than I probably should admit, I'm a below-average but enthusiastic badminton player, and I keep a small notebook of half-finished side project ideas. I also volunteer occasionally with a local youth tutoring group, which has taught me more about patience than any lecture has.

What matters to me

I care a lot about doing ordinary things properly — showing up on time, writing clean code even for small assignments, and being someone people can rely on in a group project. None of that is glamorous, but it's the version of myself I'm trying to build.
