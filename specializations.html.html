Post-DPT Specializations & Postgraduate Degrees

After completing DPT, graduates can pursue 2-year MS/MPhil and PhD programs in specialized sub-disciplines.

Upon completing the 5-year entry-level DPT, physical therapists can specialize in specific sub-domains through Master of Science (MS) or MPhil programs:

Orthopedic Physical Therapy

Focuses on fractures, joint disorders, spinal dysfunctions, osteoarthritis, and post-arthroplasty rehab.

Neurological Physical Therapy

Treatment of stroke, spinal cord injury, traumatic brain injury, Parkinson's disease, and stroke care.

Sports Physical Therapy

Injury prevention, bio-mechanical analysis, athletic conditioning, and return-to-sport rehab.

Cardiopulmonary PT

Rehabilitation for post-CABG, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), and cardiac recovery.

Pediatric Physical Therapy

Managing developmental delays, cerebral palsy, and congenital physical anomalies in children.

Geriatric Physical Therapy

Addressing age-related degenerative disorders, balance disorders, and osteoporosis management.
