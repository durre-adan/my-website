Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: What is the total duration of the DPT program?
A: The program lasts 5 academic years divided into 10 semesters.

Q: Is DPT recognized by the HEC?
A: Yes, HEC sets the uniform national curriculum for entry-level DPT programs.
