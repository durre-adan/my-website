Current State of DPT Education & Academic Institutions

DPT education is now offered across public and private sector medical universities in Pakistan.

Today, DPT is one of the most sought-after medical degree programs following MBBS and BDS in Pakistan. With over 150 universities offering the degree, thousands of students graduate annually equipped with theoretical knowledge and clinical practice hours.

Admission Prerequisites & Standards

  • Qualification: Higher Secondary School Certificate (F.Sc Pre-Medical) or A-Levels equivalent.
  • Minimum Marks: 60% aggregate score in F.Sc Pre-Medical.
  • Program Duration: 5 Years (10 Semesters).
  • Credit Hours: 175+ Total Credit Hours including clinical rotation.

Key Public Sector Institutions

King Edward Medical University (KEMU), University of Health Sciences (UHS), JPMC Karachi, Khyber Medical University (KMU).

Key Private Sector Institutions

Riphah International University, Isra University, Qarshi University, Foundation University.
