Future Trajectory & Technological Integration

The integration of artificial intelligence, wearable devices, and robotics is reshaping rehabilitation science.

The future of physical therapy in Pakistan centers on technological innovation, expanded clinical autonomy, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Emerging Innovations in Physical Therapy

Telerehabilitation

Enabling remote consultations and video-guided physical exercise for rural and underserved populations.

AI Motion Analysis

Utilizing computer vision to track joint angles, gait patterns, and muscle firing sequences in real time.

Robotic Exoskeletons

Assisting spinal cord injury and stroke patients to regain mobility through motorized lower-limb orthoses.

VR Rehabilitation

Virtual reality gamification to boost patient engagement during motor recovery exercises.
