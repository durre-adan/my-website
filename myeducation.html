What I'm studying now

I'm working toward a degree in Computer Science, with coursework spanning data structures, databases, and — most relevantly to this page — web technologies. This site itself is a submission for that last module, built from scratch with semantic HTML5 and a single hand-written stylesheet.

Outside required courses, I've been slowly teaching myself more about front-end design, mostly by rebuilding pages I like the look of and figuring out why they work.

Subjects I've enjoyed most

Data Structures for the puzzle-solving, Technical Writing for forcing me to explain things clearly, and — somewhat unexpectedly — a first-year Statistics module that made me much more comfortable reading numbers critically instead of taking them at face value.
