My Family

Most of who I am traces back to the people in this section. Here's a little about the household I grew up in and the relatives who still keep it running.

My parents

I'm the second of three children. My father works in logistics and has a habit of explaining everything, from traffic patterns to grocery shopping, like a supply chain problem. My mother teaches at a local school and is the reason I finish what I start — she has a low tolerance for half-done homework, which turned out to be a useful trait to inherit.

Weeknight dinners at home were, and still are, loud, opinionated, and usually about something completely unrelated to school.

Siblings and extended family

My older sister studies pharmacy two cities away, and we mostly stay in touch through an ongoing group chat that somehow never runs out of things to argue about. My younger brother is still in school and currently convinced he'll be a professional footballer, which the rest of the family finds equal parts endearing and concerning.

Grandparents on both sides live close enough for regular Sunday visits, and most family gatherings still happen at my grandmother's house — partly out of tradition, partly because her cooking hasn't been matched by anyone since.

Small traditions

Nothing especially grand — birthdays always come with the same slightly-burnt cake recipe, and every school report card gets read aloud at dinner, good or bad. It's the small, repeated things that end up mattering most.
