Practical Applications & Work Settings

Physical therapists operate across a range of clinical and non-clinical environments.

Physical therapists evaluate, diagnose, and treat physical movement disorders using tailored therapeutic exercises, manual manipulation, and physical modalities.

Diverse Practice Settings

Tertiary Care Hospitals

Managing OPDs, ICUs, post-surgical recovery wards, and burn units.

Private Physical Therapy Clinics

Independent autonomous practice offering personalized rehabilitation and manual therapy.

Sports Academies & Forces

Serving in national sports boards, cricket/football clubs, and Pakistan Armed Forces medical units.

Special Education Institutions

Providing pediatric therapy for children with cerebral palsy, autism, and congenital disorders.
